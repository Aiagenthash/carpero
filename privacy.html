Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 | Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Carpero Inc. ("Carpero", "we", "us", or "our"), operating at carpero.com, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, request an autonomous revenue recovery audit, or interact with our conversational voice systems.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out a form or request an audit on carpero.com, we collect the information you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, professional work email address, and direct mobile phone number.
  • Business Details: Company name, website URL, estimated monthly revenue range, and service territory.
  • Call & Interaction Data: When calling our live AI demo phone lines, call recordings and transcripts are processed solely for diagnostic demonstration, quality evaluation, and appointment setting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your collected information strictly to:

  • Deliver your requested 15-Minute Revenue Leak Audit report and analysis.
  • Confirm, schedule, and send reminders for scheduled walkthrough demo calls.
  • Send operational text (SMS) notifications and critical service updates regarding your inquiry.
  • Provide customer service and respond to your direct questions.

3. Strict Mobile Information & SMS Privacy Policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

Your phone number and SMS consent will strictly be used by Carpero Inc. to send you notifications, audit findings, and appointment updates that you have explicitly requested. We never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your phone number to any lead brokers, marketing networks, or external vendors.

4. Text Messaging (SMS) Terms & Opt-Out

By submitting your phone number on our website forms, you consent to receive informational and transactional text messages from Carpero regarding your audit request and demo appointments.

  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your appointments and requests (typically 1–3 messages per requested audit/appointment).
  • Message & data rates: Standard carrier message and data rates may apply.
  • How to opt-out: You can cancel SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. Upon receiving your STOP request, we will send one final confirmation text and cease all SMS communications immediately.
  • Support: For help or assistance, reply HELP to any message or email us directly at hello@carpero.com.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures—including TLS encryption in transit and SOC-2 compliant cloud storage—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
