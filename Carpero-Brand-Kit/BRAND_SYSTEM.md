## 1. Executive Brand Essence

### What is Carpero?
Most high-growth companies focus 95% of their budget on lead acquisition while **silently losing 20% to 40% of their pipeline** to unworked leads, failed payment dunning, and abandoned checkout flows.

**Carpero** deploys turnkey, autonomous revenue recovery engines that plug those leaks—resurrecting ghosted pipeline and recapturing lost cash without client sales reps lifting a finger.

### Core Value Propositions
* **Relentless ROI:** We are purely outcome-driven. We measure our impact in hard dollars recovered.
* **Autonomous Engineering:** AI agent loops and automated webhooks that operate 24/7/365 in the background.
* **High-EQ Persuasion:** Never spammy. Conversational, human-first outreach that preserves and enhances brand goodwill.

## 2. Master Logo Specification

The official Carpero identity is **The Mobius Recovery Loop** — an interlocking 3D tubular ribbon forming the letter **'C'**.
* **Emerald Ribbon (`#00F59B`):** Represents restored liquidity, cash flow growth, and green P&L.
* **Cyan Ribbon (`#00D2FF`):** Represents autonomous intelligence, precision engineering, and software automation.
* **Interlocking Loop:** Represents closed-loop revenue recapture — every leaked dollar is captured and looped back into growth.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

Import the official fonts in HTML:
```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@600;700;800;900&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@500;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

* **Display / Headlines:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, font-weights `700`, `800`, `900`. Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`.
* **Body & UI:** `Inter`, font-weights `400` (Regular), `500` (Medium), `600` (Semi-Bold).
* **Data & Financial Metrics:** `JetBrains Mono`, font-weight `700` (for figures like `+$84,200 RECOVERED`).

## 5. The 3 Core Offer Pillars (Service Matrix)

1. **Pillar 1: Pipeline Lead Resurrection**
   * *Problem:* Inbound leads go cold after 3–5 days and sales reps stop following up.
   * *Carpero Solution:* Autonomous multi-channel AI re-engagement sequences that resurrect dead leads, handle objections, and rebook qualified calls directly into the calendar.
2. **Pillar 2: Silent Churn & Dunning Recovery**
   * *Problem:* Credit cards expire, banks decline recurring billing, and customers churn passively without intending to.
   * *Carpero Solution:* Smart-retry routing, SMS/WhatsApp frictionless update links, and proactive dunning interception.
3. **Pillar 3: High-Intent Abandonment Intercept**
   * *Problem:* Prospects drop off at checkout, demo booking, or proposal review.
   * *Carpero Solution:* 90-second behavioral intercept triggers that offer personalized assistance and close the sale.
